06 / THE MATH BEHIND THE BUNK

NO GUESSWORK

Attendance % = Attended ÷ Total × 100
Safe skips = floor(Attended ÷ Target − Total)
Recovery lectures = ceil((Target × Total − Attended) ÷ (1 − Target))
Minimum present count = ceil(Target × Total)
Minimum future attendance = ceil(Target × Final Total − Current Attended)

Calculations assume each lecture counts equally. Your institution's official attendance rules always take priority.
